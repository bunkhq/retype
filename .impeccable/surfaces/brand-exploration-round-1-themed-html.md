# Surface brief: brand identity exploration

Scope: one shareable page presenting three complete identity directions (name, mark, palette, type, voice, app-in-context) for the unnamed local, lossless desktop converter. Mode: Experience (each board is the work; the shell recedes).
Audience: the founder/team choosing a direction. Success: each direction is recognisable with words removed, and each states the product's two truths (local, lossless remux).
User-pinned: three directions on one page, names proposed. The roll fills the slots: assigned grounded (Enclose), Impeccable's pick (Transship), competitive challenger fused (Livery). Declined challengers donate raises to the assigned direction and appear as a compact row.
Unresolved: chosen name, trademark clearance, OS priority.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Remux is a new wrapper around untouched contents. Each board dramatises one wrapper (security envelope, ISO container, race livery). Refuses the category default: gradient squircle with two arrows and "convert anything".
OWN-WORLD: Shell is quiet graphite-on-white with system type. Enclose: white stock, security-tint ink blue pattern ground, glassine window, red privacy line. Transship: oxide and slate corrugated steel, ISO 6346 stencil codes, yellow/black lashing marks. Livery: lemon body, black/white tri-stripe spine, white number roundels in heavy expanded digits.
STORY: Reader sees three wrappers, understands "same file, new wrapper, on your Mac", compares names, marks, and voice, then picks one.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Short title line, one sentence of product truth, then the first board starts at full width within the first screen: Enclose wordmark large over its security tint, live envelope swapping mp4 to mov.
