Why it fits

A window envelope separates what's inside from what's outside, which is exactly what a remux does. Security tint is the pattern printed so no one can read the letter: privacy drawn as a texture, not stated in a badge.

Risk

Envelopes mean email to most people. The mark has to stay a window envelope, never a flap, or it reads as a mail app.

Other names

Sleeve · Readdress · Encl.

Rules it lives by

  • One axis. Old and new mirror across the fold, nothing else on the line.
  • One bright thing. Only the result window is lit; the old envelope dims.
  • State by form. An empty window shows tint; a full one shows the file. No status colours needed.
  • One file in hand. Specs set in Courier, figures aligned.

Transship to .mov

Every conversion gets a manifest line. "Intact" means not one frame was re-encoded.

Why it fits

mp4 and mov are literally called containers. Transshipment is moving cargo from one vessel to another at the port without unpacking it. The seal gives lossless a visible, checkable symbol.

Risk

Containers are the most familiar metaphor in software. Docker owns the whale and the box. This is also the coldest of the three for everyday users. It wins with editors who know what a container is.

The verb carries the brand: you respray a file. The stripe direction is the only motion the brand uses.

Why it fits

A livery is a new skin over an unchanged machine, and racing liveries are identity systems built to work at speed. Giving every format its own livery turns the file browser into a visible system.

Risk

The loudest of the three. Motorsport energy can feel like a speed promise, and re-encodes are not fast. Copy has to keep "engine swap" honest. The colourways stay original: no borrowed sponsor liveries.

Other names

Respray · Pitwall · Wrap

Before you choose

  1. Check the names. None of these has been searched for trademarks, App Store conflicts, or domains.
  2. Pick a wrapper, not a palette. The colours can move. The metaphor sets the voice, the verb in Finder, and the icon.
  3. Next step after a pick: a full design system, production app icons at every macOS size, and the first app screens.

Also explored, then folded into Enclose as rules:

  • Formal garden plans. Too ornamental. Kept the single axis that old and new mirror across.
  • Bioluminescent forest. Off-subject. Kept one brightness reserved for the result.
  • Console menu in a void. Too game-like. Kept objects on a ground with no panels around them.
  • Studio patch bay. A tool costume. Kept state drawn by form instead of colour.
  • Particle event display. Too dense. Kept one file in hand and aligned figures.
