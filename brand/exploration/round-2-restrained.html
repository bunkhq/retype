Notes

The familiar version. There is also the category standard: system font, blue accent, sidebar, and a gradient icon with two arrows. It would sit comfortably next to Permute, CloudConvert, and HandBrake. It's safe, and it would look like all of them. I can build it out if you want that baseline.

  1. Names are unchecked. None has been searched for trademarks, App Store conflicts, or domains.
  2. "Same quality" in the demo assumes the video files hold standard H.264 and AAC, so changing the container needs no re-encode.
  3. Mix and match. Each name, interaction, and palette can move to another board if one pairing clicks.
